# Results (77 words)

Across nine user-selected clips, the shared temporal representation was ready in 66 of 574 perception frames (11.5%) and at 4 assumed clip endpoints. Rules produced 4 endpoint decisions; the LLM produced 20 valid outputs from 20 repeated requests. In each of three operational repetitions, rules delivered 5/9 accepted decisions and the LLM delivered 0/9. Survey alignment remains unverified, and the response workbook was unavailable; human preference and appropriateness comparisons therefore remain unresolved. No physical behaviour was evaluated.

# Conclusion (55 words)

The pipeline provides a shared, inspectable representation for both policies, but usable evidence and timely client acceptance constrain decision availability. This development replay cannot establish which policy better matches human preferences. Future evaluation needs the survey workbook, verified video synchronisation, improved cue coverage, timely delivery and new live encounters, including measurements of physically executed behaviour.
